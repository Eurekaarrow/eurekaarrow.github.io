I am pursuing the MS in Artificial Intelligence at Northwestern University. I received my BSc in Computer Science with First Class Honours from Hong Kong Baptist University, Zhuhai.

Previously I was a research intern with the XPixel Group, MMLab at the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Chao Dong, and at the Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Yilun Chen. I was also a summer visiting student in AI and Machine Learning at the University of Oxford, advised by Prof. Tom Rainforth.

I work closely with Dr. Wei Ji at Yale and Dr. Shihao Zou at SIAT.

Research

I am interested in studying intelligence by building multimodal systems that learn and develop through experience and interaction with the world.

My current work asks how such systems can expand what they perceive, internalize what they experience, and use their understanding to reason about and act in the world.

Perception beyond human senses
